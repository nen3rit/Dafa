Daily Cooking Tip

The Power of Salt Flushing for Enhanced Flavor

Before cooking vegetables, try 'salt flushing' them by soaking in a heavily salted brine (1/4 cup salt to 4 cups water) for 15-30 minutes. This process breaks down the cellular structure of the veggies, allowing them to absorb flavors better while also enhancing their natural sweetness and reducing bitterness. Rinse and dry before cooking for an optimal texture and enhanced flavor profile that most home cooks overlook.
